# UI layout A Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkboxes.

**Goal:** Rebuild the screens as conversation (left, 48%) and summary sheet (right), starting in soft mint.

**Architecture:** `App.tsx` keeps its step machine and now owns the conversation history. `Chat` draws the left panel for every step; `Sheet` draws the right. No API or rule changes.

**Tech Stack:** React 19, Tailwind 4 `@theme` tokens, `@fontsource-variable/noto-sans` and `-tamil`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-28-ui-layout-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- Text 16px or more; tap targets 48px or more; no sideways scroll at 360px; two columns from 800px, tabs below.
- Consent before anything goes to Sarvam. Nothing evaluated before confirm. Never change an answer silently.
- Suggestions show checks, conflicts, sources, review label; no-guarantee line stays in view; amounts never added.
- Only visible answers are sent (`toFacts`). No browser storage, except the colour choice added later. The chat design and themes were added during the build; see the spec.

## Review Focus
- "Change" while the eligibility check is running: the late reply must be ignored.
- "Change" after results: results and suggestions are cleared until confirmed again.
- "Change" during a voice conversation: the question is asked again with the microphone.
- Suggest call fails: the full results show.
- No key: tap journey works, shows suggestions, no voice buttons, nothing is spoken.
