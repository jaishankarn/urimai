# Benefits research: location, questions to ask and more benefits

Collected on 28 Nov 2026. Read with [use-cases.md](use-cases.md). **Nothing is verified against a government order.** This is not a complete list of government benefits: it covers our six stories.

## Does location change the benefits?

**What we found:** the main pensions are state and national schemes, listed as single services on e-Sevai. We found **no evidence of a district-specific pension rule**. District pages do disagree, but several are years old and none we read presents a difference as a local rule. Our inference is that these are inconsistent or outdated pages. That is not proven, and 31 districts gave us no pension conditions to compare.

**Where location or rural/urban does or may matter:**

| Topic | Difference | Mark |
|---|---|---|
| Below-poverty-line number | City corporation: Corporation office. Rural: BDO or panchayat office ([Chennai page](https://chennai.nic.in/oap-eligibility/)) | [D] |
| Housing help | Separate rural (PMAY-G) and urban (PMAY-U) schemes with different conditions | [Q] |
| Disability bus travel | 75% concession on state buses [D]; a policy note reportedly gives 100% on Chennai MTC buses | [D] / [Q] |
| Child sponsorship | One income test reportedly differs: ₹72,000 rural, ₹96,000 elsewhere | [Q] |
| Offices and sign-up camps | Local offices and dated camps differ by district | [D] |

So ask for **district** and **village panchayat or town/city**. Use them to route the person to the right office and to choose housing and other location-based rules. Do not change pension results by district unless an official order says so.

## What to ask

For the widow story, **her husband's work and how he died open up benefits that location does not.** Ask one at a time, and only what is needed.

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do you live in Tamil Nadu? Which district? Village or town/city? | State schemes, office, housing |
| 2 | How old are you? | Widow and old-age pension age rules |
| 3 | When did your husband die, and how old was he? | Family benefit, insurance, claim deadlines |
| 4 | Was it an accident? Did it happen at work? | Accident amounts are higher; some are accident-only |
| 5 | What work did he do? Construction, driving, other manual work, farm labour, farming, fishing, handloom, salaried job, government job? | Decides which welfare board or employer benefit |
| 6 | Was he registered with a welfare board? Do you have the card? | Board benefits are tied to registration (except as noted below) |
| 7 | Did his bank passbook show a yearly insurance deduction (₹436 or ₹20)? | PMJJBY or PMSBY. Older premiums were different [Q] |
| 8 | Was he a main earner? | National family benefit |
| 9 | Do you have income? Value of house or land? Adult son or other relatives at home? | "Destitute" and property conditions |
| 10 | Does your household have a below-poverty-line number? | National schemes |

Never ask for Aadhaar or bank account numbers. Question 7 asks only whether a deduction appears.

## Benefits tied to a death

### Tamil Nadu welfare boards
From the Labour Department [policy note 2025–26](https://labour.tn.gov.in/pdf/policy_note_lwsd_e_pn_2025_26.pdf) [O]:

| Benefit | Construction | Unorganised drivers | Manual workers and other boards |
|---|---|---|---|
| Natural death | ₹50,000 | ₹50,000 | ₹30,000 |
| Accidental death | ₹2,00,000 | ₹2,00,000 | ₹1,25,000 (fire and match workers' board ₹2,00,000) |
| Funeral expenses | ₹5,000 | ₹5,000 | ₹5,000 |
| Family pension | ₹500 a month | none listed | none listed |

- Construction work: **₹5,00,000** for accidental death at a worksite, **including unregistered workers** ([board sheet](https://tnuwwb.tn.gov.in/docs/welfare-claim.pdf), file dated Dec 2022) [O].
- e-Sevai lists the construction, manual and driver board claims as services, with a ₹60 service charge [O].
- **Not found:** membership and renewal conditions, claim deadlines, claim documents.

### Farm families, fishers, weavers
- **Farmers' protection scheme (CMUPT) members:** natural death ₹20,000, accidental death ₹1,00,000, funeral ₹2,500 ([Kanniyakumari](https://kanniyakumari.nic.in/social-security-schemes/), 2023) [D]. The page has an apparent copy error in this section.
- **Fishermen and handloom weavers:** e-Sevai lists death assistance or claim services [O]. Amounts not collected.

### Insurance through a bank account

| Scheme | Cover | Age | Yearly premium | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PMJJBY (life) | ₹2,00,000, death from any reason | 18–50 to join | ₹436 | [O](https://financialservices.gov.in/pradhan-mantri-jeevan-jyoti-bima-yojana-pmjjby) |
| PMSBY (accident) | ₹2,00,000 for accidental death | 18–70 | ₹20 | [O](https://financialservices.gov.in/pradhan-mantri-suraksha-bima-yojana-pmsby) |

Applies only if he was enrolled and covered when he died. Reported [Q]: PMJJBY cover can continue to age 55; the nominee claims; the form prefers claims within 30 days; some deaths soon after joining are excluded. Ask the bank.

### Salaried jobs [Q]
Provident fund members' families may get a widow pension and a lump-sum insurance (EPFO sources give different maximums). For deaths from work injury, ESIC may pay dependants a pension. Show only as "ask the employer or office".

### National family benefit
₹20,000 [O, see use-cases.md]. We did **not** find it by name in the e-Sevai service lists, so confirm where to apply.

## Widows, deserted, destitute and unmarried women

**Tamil Nadu Widows and Destitute Women Welfare Board** (relevant to UC1, UC4, UC5):
- Widows, deserted, destitute, vulnerable and unmarried women can register ([Coimbatore](https://coimbatore.nic.in/widows-and-destitute-women-can-register-as-members-and-avail-government-welfare-benefits/)) [D].
- Documents listed for a Sept 2026 camp: Aadhaar, family card, widow or deserted-woman certificate, photo, income certificate, residence certificate, mobile number [D]. The camp date has passed.
- Areas named: education, health, jobs, self-help groups, vocational training ([Nilgiris](https://nilgiris.nic.in/p-r-no432-new-registration-of-details-on-the-website-by-widows-destitute-women-and-unmarried-women-of-advanced-age/)) [D].
- One district page says members who take certain self-employment bank loans get ₹50,000 from the Board towards their own contribution ([Chengalpattu](https://chengalpattu.nic.in/departments/social-welfare/)) [D].
- **Not found:** a full list of benefits.

**If a widow's daughter is marrying:** ₹25,000 or ₹50,000 (by the bride's education) plus 8 g of gold ([Kancheepuram](https://kancheepuram.nic.in/departments/social-welfare/), updated Mar 2026) [D]. Older district pages list 4 g, and some give an income limit of ₹72,000. Confirm it is still open.

**Children (Mission Vatsalya sponsorship)** [Q]: ₹4,000 a month per child; the national guideline includes children whose mother is widowed, divorced or abandoned. Income limits and Tamil Nadu process unconfirmed; a district committee approves. Relevant to UC4.

**Housing** [Q]: urban PMAY gives widows preference among families without a permanent house. Rural district notices list widows as a priority, with waiting-list and land-document conditions.

## Disability: national aids and appliances (UC2) [Q]
ADIP: 40% disability with UDID. Reported full subsidy for monthly family income up to ₹22,500 and half for ₹22,501–30,000; up to ₹30,000 for high-end artificial limbs. Sources show different income limits.

A recent update date does not prove the rules on a page are current.

## How the app should use this
1. Keep **one statewide rule set** for pensions, with district used for routing.
2. Store a mark, source link and checked date with every fact.
3. Use **[O] and [D] facts only** for matching. Show [Q] items, if at all, as "ask the office about this".
4. Where sources disagree on a condition that changes the result, answer **"needs more information"**.
5. Never add benefit amounts together.
