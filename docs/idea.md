URIMAI - Your Voice. Your Rights. Your Benefits.

The problem
Many elderly people and financially vulnerable families in India miss out on government welfare benefits because they don't know which schemes they may qualify for, but also to complete the application process. Government forms, documentation requirements and application instructions can be difficult to understand, especially for people with limited literacy or digital skills. As a result, many start applications but abandon them midway or depend on others to complete them.

Who has this problem, and where?
This particularly affects elderly people, widows and low-income families across rural and semi-urban India. The challenge often begins when their circumstances change, such as losing a family member, reaching retirement age or losing their source of income. 
Even after discovering a government benefit they may be entitled to, many struggle to understand the eligibility requirements, gather the necessary documents and complete the application. Renewals and documentation issues create further hurdles, often forcing them to make repeated visits to service centres or depend on relatives and intermediaries for help.

The voice-first product
Urimai is a multilingual, voice-first government benefits companion that helps people navigate the entire journey; from discovering benefits they may be eligible for to understanding requirements, completing application forms and following through with the process.
A person can simply describe their circumstances in their own words. Urimai asks relevant questions, checks their answers against verified government eligibility rules and identifies benefits they may qualify for.
But it doesn't stop there. It explains required documents and actively helps people fill out application forms through conversation. Instead of typing into complicated forms, users can answer questions by speaking in their own language, and Urimai converts their responses into the relevant form fields. It explains unfamiliar questions, identifies missing information and reads back completed details for confirmation.
Once the application is completed, Urimai helps users review and verify all the information, prepares the application for submission and assists them through the submission process using the appropriate official channel. It keeps track of their progress, pending actions and required follow-ups. If someone gets stuck or has to leave midway, Urimai saves everything and picks up exactly where they stopped when they return, guiding them through the remaining steps until the application process is complete.

Why voice comes first
The people who need this help may struggle to read lengthy instructions, type information or navigate complicated websites. Voice allows them to describe their circumstances, answer questions, provide information for application forms and receive step-by-step assistance naturally in their own language.
Instead of reading and filling out complicated forms themselves, they can simply speak their answers while Urimai converts them into the relevant fields, explains unfamiliar questions, identifies missing information and helps them complete each step. They can review, correct and confirm their information through conversation, without having to navigate complex digital processes alone.
With Urimai, accessing government support begins with something everyone should be able to do: simply speaking.
